Most word frequency data are derived from large text corpora, and thus measure how often a word is used in language - or the purely linguistic experience with a word. For the Flickr word frequency norms, we instead harvested image tags from Flickr, a large picture sharing website. Here, the words are thus inherently tied to extra-linguistic experience, lying at the intersection between linguistic and visual experience, with specific biases induced by being based on image-focused social media.

[**Link to the dataset**](https://osf.io/2zfs3/)

More information can be found here:

[Petilli, M. A., Günther, F., & Marelli, M. (2024). The Flickr frequency norms: what 17 years of images tagged online tell us about lexical processing. *Behavior Research Methods*, *56*(1), 126-147.](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-022-02031-y)
