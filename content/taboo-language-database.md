This database contains taboo language (taboo words and multiword-expressions, including swear words, slurs, insults, ...), collected in 13 different languages and 17 countries. The database consists of two components:

- A collection of taboo language produced by native speakers
- Participant ratings on the most prominent expressions in each language on six dimensions: Concreteness, valence, arousal, age of acquisition, tabooness, and offensiveness

[**Link to the dataset**](https://osf.io/ecr32)

For more information, see the open access article by [Sulpizio, Günther, et al. (2024)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-024-02376-6).
