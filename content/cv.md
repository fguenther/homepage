### Academic Career

I received my PhD in Cognitive Science in 2017 at the University of Tübingen, under the supervision of Barbara Kaup.

Title of my dissertation: [*The Cognitive Relevance of Distributional Semantic Models*](https://bibliographie.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/handle/10900/78506)

In July 2018, the University of Tübingen awarded a Doctoral Thesis Award to my dissertation

In June 2019, my dissertation got awarded 3rd place at the Dissertation contest of the German Psychological Society (DGPs), section General Psychology

I completed my habilitation in 2026 at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin.

Title of my habilitation: [*Building and extrapolating concept representations from experience*](https://edoc.hu-berlin.de/items/7391b563-50f3-4069-bbfd-6185ef64a60b)
